Texas's 2026 Reload: How the Georgia Loss Lit the Fuse

Georgia has been the wall. Kirby Smart's Bulldogs went 2–0 against Texas in 2024 and beat them again 35–10 in November 2025. Rather than tweak, head coach Steve Sarkisian ran a full schematic and roster overhaul across the 2026 offseason — targeting the trenches, the coaching staff, and physical depth to close the gap.

1. The Coaching Counter

The most direct shot at Smart was hiring SEC veteran Will Muschamp as defensive coordinator.

  • Muschamp was a key architect of Georgia's back-to-back national championship defenses.
  • He knows Smart's schematic vulnerabilities and tendencies firsthand.
  • His aggressive, turn-them-loose style is meant to replicate the physical presence Georgia used to overpower Texas.

2. Matching Georgia's Trench Brutality

Georgia has repeatedly won by dominating the line of scrimmage. Texas answered by reloading both lines with size.

  • Offensive line: returning starters plus portal depth in Melvin Siani and Laurence Seymore to hold up in the fourth quarter.
  • Defensive interior: transfers next to edge rusher Colin Simmons to collapse the pocket.

3. Erasing the Blue-Chip Gap

Texas treated the transfer portal as an equalizer — adding downhill enforcers to match Georgia's physicality rather than out-recruit it in a single cycle.

4. Unleashing Arch

Arch Manning is QB1. The offense leans into more RPO and horizontal space to keep Georgia's linebackers out of the backfield.
